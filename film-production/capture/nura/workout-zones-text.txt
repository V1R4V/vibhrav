Understand this workout
What this session did for you

You spent 21 minutes above 80% of your heart-rate range. Time up there is what raises VO2 max and top-end fitness, and it is also what costs the most recovery. It scored 13.4 of 21 on its own; your day’s strain adds everything else you did.

How this workout is scored

Each heart-rate reading is placed between your resting rate and your maximum, and weighted so that hard minutes count far more than easy ones. The sum is the heart-rate load (110), which maps onto the 0 to 21 strain scale.

Calories are Apple’s estimate, shown with a ±20% band for this kind of activity.

What makes a session count for more
More minutes in zones 4 and 5.
A longer session at the same intensity.
Shorter rests between hard efforts.
How to read it

Compare this session with others of the same activity rather than across sports: wrist heart rate behaves differently in each. If the second half ran much higher than the first at the same pace, you were tiring or dehydrating; that drift shrinks as fitness improves.
