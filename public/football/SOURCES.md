The Bernabéu scene is an original procedural architectural study: silver horizontal louvres, rounded rectangular envelope, open roof, blue seating and pitch. Reference: https://www.realmadrid.com/en-US/the-club/history/stadium/santiago-bernabeu and https://www.realmadrid.com/es-ES/santiago-bernabeu/nuevo-estadio/santiago-bernabeu-siglo-xxi. It is interpretive, not a surveyed or official architectural model. The poster is captured from that same local scene for no-JavaScript, reduced-motion and WebGL-failure views.
