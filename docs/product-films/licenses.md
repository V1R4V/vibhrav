Music source files and mixed audio authoring assets stay in ignored local
production storage. Only the completed audiovisual films are distributed on the
website. Do not publish music-only files or claim ownership of the tracks.
Variable font source URLs and runtime checksums appear in the asset manifest.

Official Remotion skills were read from their primary source as instructions;
no skill installers or arbitrary scripts were run. Shotcraft's Apache2.0 sources
were reviewed as recipes, not installed/copied/executed. Poly Haven/R3F/Drei were
evaluated; all unnecessary independent 3D objects were removed, so no HDRI or
third-party 3D asset is used in the new films.
