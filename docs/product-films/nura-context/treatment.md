# Nura — Behind the number

A new, standalone 54-second brand film. Existing three films and portfolio playback remain unchanged pending review.

## Product truth
Nura puts Apple Watch data into daily and longer-term context. The film explains the actual four-pillar health estimate, differentiates daily from trailing-28-day constructs, shows an actual seeded workout trace and time-in-zone distribution, and introduces recovery against the person's baseline. It does not imply medical validation or causal relationships. Recovery is separate from the health score. No screenshot-led scenes, fake application screens, or invented controls.

## Creative direction
A physical, open ring is a lens through which the data becomes understandable. Anodized green and pearl ceramic arcs, precise engraved divisions, broad studio reflections. Pine-black and warm ivory alternate deliberately. Lavender identifies sleep; muted peach identifies effort. The ring separates to reveal the four contributors, unrolls into a time landscape, becomes a workout contour, and resolves back into Nura's open-circle mark. Type remains crisp and level above the dimensional world. No stock tech tunnel, decorative particle field, or interchangeable template panels.
