# Reference register and critical adaptation

Research accessed 22 November 2026. Launchscene supplies scene annotations and
representative CDN frames. The frame montage was visually inspected; full films
were not watched/listened to here. Sound and camera descriptions from the scene
annotations are treated as reference metadata, not firsthand audiovisual review.
Reference frames are research only, never footage in our films.

## Technical resources reviewed

- [Official Remotion skills](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills): router,
  creation, layout, markup, timing, images, local fonts, sequencing, transitions,
  multi-scene structure, editable timelines, audio and rendering. Read from
  primary source; no arbitrary skill scripts executed or broad skills installed.
- [Remotion docs](https://www.remotion.dev/docs): frame-derived animation,
  explicit scene durations, static local assets, reproducible rendering.
- [Playwright screenshots](https://playwright.dev/docs/screenshots): deterministic
  viewport/DPR, loaded fonts, native interaction state, PNG provenance.
- [Shotcraft](https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft): reviewed Apache-2.0
  license and three recipe sources (depth layers, row embedding, subtractive
  outro). No installation/execution and no copied implementation. Adopt the
  principle of returning to a precise landing position; reject flying rows and
  publish-style theatrics where they imply actions these products do not do.
- [Remotion Three](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/three),
  [R3F](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/), [Drei](https://drei.docs.pmnd.rs/) and
  [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/): evaluated for dimensional scenes.
  Decision: no independent 3D object contributes enough to this story. Use
  restrained, flat native surfaces and gentle compositing depth instead. No
  geometry/material/HDRI dependencies are needed in these replacements.

## Editorial decision

The references establish hierarchy, information continuity and timing, not a
visual template to clone. Nura uses green analytical space; StudyFlow uses blue
and an explicit causal workflow; Vyaya uses editorial serif contrast with its
own violet identity and category colors. Most reading shots are frontal. Camera
moves end before the important reading beat. No imported reference footage,
trademark impersonation, particle fields, random primitives or heavy blur.
