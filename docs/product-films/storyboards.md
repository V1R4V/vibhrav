# New scripts, treatment and storyboards

Written after the initial audit. Copy describes verified native capabilities;
precise captured dates and numeric values live in each capture manifest.
All native captures are fictional, engine/source-derived demonstrations. The
film never labels them personal records or clinically validated conclusions.

## Nura — 46 seconds

**Definition:** Nura organizes Apple Watch health readings, activity and derived
estimates into daily and longer-term analytics.
**Need:** a useful relationship between today's readings, several weeks of data,
and the sessions behind activity totals.
**Promise:** clearer context with inspectable inputs; no health-outcome guarantee.
**Three features:** unified daily overview → period/trend context → workouts and
historical exploration. Sleep and recovery support the close.
**Tone:** considered, precise, quietly curious. Forest green; dashboard-led.
**Treatment:** the native Summary health card opens the film; the camera settles
on it rather than a decorative object. Scope changes are labeled and cut, never
animated as if daily and period scores were the same number. Graphs are actual
captured data. Workouts use real navigation, not nonexistent zone cross-links.

## StudyFlow — 44 seconds

**Definition:** a subject-based planner, focus timer and study-session record.
**Need:** turn an intended study week into work that is recorded and reviewable.
**Promise:** plans, timed sessions and progress stay connected.
**Three features:** weekly plan → subject timer/reflection → saved progress/log.
**Tone:** purposeful, direct, warm. Blue and ink. Male narration retained.
**Treatment:** one Friday Algorithms session connects every shot; no unrelated
setup tour. The actual native 45-minute save changes 9h45 to 10h30 weekly time,
and goal credit 69% to75%. The elapsed session is compressed editorially; never
present a 45-minute countdown occurring in six real-world seconds.

## Vyaya — 34 seconds

**Definition:** a spending and income tracker with three distinct money lanes.
**Need:** identify ordinary spending without funding and repayments obscuring it.
**Promise:** see tracked income, purchases, categories and resulting net clearly.
**Three features:** income/category view → transaction detail/entry → lane isolation.
**Tone:** composed, editorial, concrete. Native violet and category colors;
serif punctuation consistent with Vyaya's identity.
**Treatment:** use the actual Sankey rather than procedural money shapes. A real
$5.25 cafe entry changes spending/net exactly once. Three lanes are shown using
shipped dashboard controls late in the film. Net means tracked income minus
tracked expense, not an independently verified account balance.

## Muted version and caption policy

On-screen headlines and persistent feature labels communicate each sequence
without audio. Exact voice cues are measured after synthesis, then exported as
WebVTT; they are not estimated from character count. No on-frame provenance,
production badges, personal names or “real app UI” labels. Honest time-jump
labels and distinct score-window labels convey meaningful product context.
